## Step 7: rfr-creation

- Built from the logo and intro-film trials (archive pull request #278, and `docs/brand/` and `docs/video/intro/` there): stages that lock each artifact before a costlier one, checks that are technical conformance only, and taste left to the person.
- A review page of options at real sizes as the review surface; it worked for the logo and the brand, one short reply a round.
- Declared outside services (image and video generation) with what each is for and its limits; and the process half that is not yet built: discovery recommends a project's services, the person approves them, and the list is kept up as the project goes.
- It is the second set of stages for `rfr-process`, after `rfr-engineering`, so the test that the engine is domain-neutral. What it cannot express goes back to Core as a change to the engine.
